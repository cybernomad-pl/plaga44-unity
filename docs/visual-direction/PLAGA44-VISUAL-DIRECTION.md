# PLAGA'44 - VISUAL DIRECTION DOCUMENT

**Created:** 2025-10-30
**Team:** VISUAL CONTENT (Wojciech Siudmak, HR Giger, Grzegorz Rosinski, Glenn Fabry, Ridley Scott, Sigourney Weaver, Michael Biehn)
**Purpose:** Art direction, visual themes, concept descriptions, cinematic guidance for PLAGA'44 VR game
**Project:** CYBERNOMAD/PLAGA'44 - Post-Multi-Apocalypse Survival RPG, Polska 2144

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

PLAGA'44 visual identity is built on THREE VISUAL WORLDS colliding:

1. **ORBITAL STERILITY** (neZUS stations) - Clinical white, chrome, glass. 2001: Space Odyssey meets medical dystopia.
2. **ONTOLOGICAL HORROR** (The Infected) - HR Giger biomechanical decay. Body remains, self erases. Touch = death.
3. **FLOODED WARSAW 2144** (Surface world) - Blade Runner meets Metro 2033. Vertical architecture, water everywhere, neon + rust.

**Core visual philosophy:** Beauty in decay, hope in ruins, humanity in prosthetics.

---

## I. OVERALL ART DIRECTION (Wojciech Siudmak)

### Visual Pillars

**1. POLSKA 2144 AESTHETIC**
- NOT generic post-apocalypse (no Mad Max desert)
- IS: Flooded Gothic city, vertical survival, Slavic brutalism meets organic decay
- Color palette: Cold blues (water), warm ambers (firelight), sickly greens (infected zones), sterile whites (neZUS)
- Lighting: Overcast perpetual twilight on surface, harsh clinical on orbit, bioluminescence in flooded ruins

**2. THREE-LAYER VISUAL SEPARATION**

**LAYER 1: ORBIT (neZUS stations)**
- Style: Kubrick's 2001 + THX-1138 sterility
- Colors: White (#FFFFFF), chrome (#C0C0C0), glass transparency, clinical blue (#E0F7FF)
- Lighting: Harsh, even, no shadows (medical environment)
- Materials: Polished metal, tempered glass, medical plastics
- Mood: Unsettling perfection, inhuman precision, corporate immortality

**LAYER 2: INFECTED ZONES (COVID-X horror)**
- Style: HR Giger biomechanical + David Cronenberg body horror
- Colors: Sickly greens (#3D5A3D), bruised purples (#4A2A4A), necrotic grays (#6B6B6B)
- Lighting: Dim, shadows hide threats, bioluminescent patches (infected tissue glows faintly)
- Materials: Organic decay, wet surfaces, pulsing biomass
- Mood: Dread, revulsion, existential terror (you could BECOME this)

**LAYER 3: FLOODED WARSAW (surface survival)**
- Style: Blade Runner + Metro 2033 + The Witcher 3 (Slavic texture)
- Colors: Rust reds (#8B4513), waterlogged grays (#4A5F6A), neon accents (cyan #00FFFF, magenta #FF00FF)
- Lighting: Perpetual overcast, shafts of light through clouds, firelight, jury-rigged electric lights
- Materials: Corroded metal, rotting wood, repurposed tech, moss-covered concrete
- Mood: Melancholy beauty, stubborn survival, history drowned but not forgotten

**3. VISUAL MOTIFS (recurring themes)**

- **Water as constant presence:** Rain, flooding, reflections, rust. Water = death zone (permadeath). Visual language: stillness = danger.
- **Verticality:** Rooftops, towers, climbing. Humanity survives UP, not OUT.
- **Prosthetics as identity:** Every NPC has visible augmentation. Bodies are patchwork histories.
- **Polish symbols half-submerged:** Eagle emblems on drowned buildings, Solidarity graffiti fading underwater, church spires as navigation landmarks.
- **Orbital surveillance:** Drones, satellites, the ever-present "eye in the sky" (neZUS watching).

## II. CHARACTER CREATION - VISUAL PHASES

### PHASE 0: LIMBO (Bezcielesna perspektywa)

**Visual Concept:**
- Floating POV in circular room, 12 white medical pods arranged in mandala pattern
- Glass ceiling above: STARS (vast, cold, indifferent) and EARTH curvature (cyan-blue, cloud-swirled)
- Fog/mist obscures walls - only pods in focus (dreamlike isolation)
- Lighting: Soft, diffused from above (godlight through glass ceiling)
- Silence broken only by: breath sounds, heartbeat bass hum, life-support ambient drone

**Key visual beats:**
1. **Opening shot:** Camera/POV high above pods, slow descent (birth/death metaphor)
2. **Pod details:** Frosted glass lids, bodies visible as silhouettes (gender/build discernible, no faces)
3. **Choice moment:** Selected pod glows faintly (warm amber), others fade to darkness
4. **Transfer effect:** POV pulled magnetically into pod (vortex/tunnel effect, white-out)

**Color palette:** White (#FFFFFF), glass blue (#E8F4F8), amber glow (#FFB347), black void (#000000)

**Reference imagery:**
- Siudmak's "Kosmos" series (metaphysical spaces)
- 2001: Space Odyssey (Star Gate sequence - controlled, not chaotic)
- The Fountain (Aronofsky) - floating in amniotic space

### PHASE 1-3: RECONSTRUCTION (Oczy, Skora, Twarz)

**Visual Concept:**
- Clinical horror - medical procedures as dehumanization
- POV restricted (can't move, only observe)
- Tools descend into frame from above (invasive, impersonal)
- Screens display colors/skin tones scrolling (like product catalog)

**Key visual beats:**

**EYES (Phase 1):**
- Extreme close-up: mechanical tool approaches eye (fish-eye lens distortion)
- Colors scroll on screen: reds, blues, greens (unnatural saturation)
- Flash of light when scanner activates (white-out, momentary blindness)
- Emerging vision: blurry at first, sharpens to reveal droid medyczny's face (neutral, non-threatening but inhuman)

**SKIN (Phase 2):**
- Wide shot: Body lying in pod, skin texture missing (smooth mannequin horror)
- Screen shows skin tones (realistic range: pale to dark, undertones visible)
- Pod machinery hums, white mist fills chamber (obscures body during "application")
- Reveal: Hands come into focus with new skin (player sees their hands for first time - uncanny)

**FACE (Phase 3):**
- Hologram floats before player: rotating 3D face model (current: blank, featureless)
- System message: "REKONSTRUKCJA TWARZY ORYGINALNEJ: NIEMOZLIWA" (red text, finality)
- Preset faces appear (diverse, but generic - loss of original identity)
- Choice locked in: hologram solidifies, overlays player's POV (brief double-vision effect)

**Color palette:** Medical white (#FFFFFF), steel blue (#4682B4), warning red (#FF6347), skin tone spectrum (realistic)

**Mood:** Sterile, unsettling, identity as commodity. Player is PRODUCT being assembled.

**Reference imagery:**
- THX-1138 (white prison of bureaucracy)
- Gattaca (genetic selection as cold procedure)
- Ghost in the Shell (cyborg assembly)

### PHASE 4: WERYFIKACJA (Tutorial gestu)

**Visual Concept:**
- Droid medyczny (Titanfall 2 style): Humanoid but clearly machine. Worn, scratched, FUNCTIONAL not pristine.
- Tablet with symbols (OCZY | SKORA | TWARZ) glows faintly - simple iconography
- Player sees own hands clearly for first time (skin, fingers, can move them)

**Key visual beats:**
1. Droid enters frame, tilts head (curiosity? assessment?)
2. Player raises hand (tutorial), sees fingers, flexes them (agency restored)
3. Thumbs-up gesture: Droid's eye-lights brighten (positive feedback)
4. Tablet symbols check off one by one (procedural progress)

**Color palette:** Droid gray (#7D7D7D), tablet blue (#1E90FF), ambient white (#F0F0F0)

**Droid design notes (Siudmak + Giger):**
- NOT sleek Apple aesthetic
- IS: Soviet-era utilitarian + wear (scratches, dents, repaired joints)
- Single glowing eye-strip (not "face" - functional sensor)
- Movement: Precise but slightly jerky (machine, not pretending to be human)

### PHASE 5-6: PROTETYKA (Wybor i instalacja)

**Visual Concept:**
- Workshop aesthetic: Tables with prosthetic limbs displayed like meat in butcher shop
- Three choices per limb: CRUDE MECH, BIO-MIMIC, ORGANIC SERUM
- Installation sequence: BODY HORROR (Stephen King + HR Giger)

**Prosthetic visual design (Giger + Fabry):**

**CRUDE MECH (Toporna mechaproteza):**
- Exposed pistons, cables, metal joints (no skin covering)
- Materials: Brushed steel, rust patches, bolts visible
- Movement: Audible servos, slight lag (mechanical delay)
- Aesthetic: Cyberpunk brutalism, function over form
- Emotional impact: SHAME (body is visibly "broken," augmented)

**BIO-MIMIC (Ludzka imitacja):**
- Synthetic skin covering (almost convincing, but uncanny valley)
- Seams visible at joints (like doll parts)
- Movement: Smooth, nearly natural (but too perfect - unsettling)
- Aesthetic: Westworld hosts, Blade Runner replicants
- Emotional impact: DENIAL (pretending to be whole, but false)

**ORGANIC SERUM (Regeneracja):**
- NO prosthetic visible initially
- Side effect: Non-dominant hand TWITCHES, spasms (uncontrolled)
- Visual: Hand curls involuntarily, fingers twitch (Cronenberg body betrayal)
- Aesthetic: Body fighting itself, organic horror
- Emotional impact: FEAR (your own body is unreliable)

**Installation sequence (Phase 6) - CRITICAL CINEMATIC MOMENT:**

**Shot progression (Ridley Scott direction):**
1. **Wide shot:** Player on surgical table, droid holding circular saw (silhouette against bright light)
2. **Close-up:** Saw blade rotating (sound design critical - metallic whine)
3. **POV:** Saw approaches limb (out of focus, edges of frame blur - panic vision)
4. **CUT TO BLACK** - player loses consciousness BEFORE graphic content (timejump mercy)
5. **Fade in:** Soft light, blurry vision clearing, limb bandaged/replaced (aftermath, not process)

**Color palette:** Surgical white (#FFFFFF), steel blade reflection (#C0C0C0), blood red (IMPLIED, not shown - bandages, not gore), fade to black (#000000)

**Haptic feedback notes:**
- Saw approaches: Controller vibrates (low rumble building to intense)
- Contact moment: SHARP burst vibration (then cut to black)
- Wake up: Gentle pulse (heartbeat rhythm in new limb)

**Critical design decision:** DO NOT SHOW GRAPHIC SURGERY. Imply through sound, vibration, shadows. Horror is in anticipation, not spectacle. Timejump preserves agency (player not forced to watch body violation).

## III. INFECTED - BIOMECHANICAL HORROR (HR Giger)

### Design Philosophy

COVID-X is ONTOLOGICAL plague, not biological zombie virus. Infected are:
- NOT rotting corpses (body intact)
- NOT mindless (reptilian brain functional - cunning, hunting)
- NOT undead (alive, but SELF is gone)

**Core horror:** You could touch them, and YOU become them. Your body continues, but YOU die.

### Visual Design

**STAGE 1: EARLY INFECTION (0-24 hours?)**
- Appearance: Nearly normal, but EYES change (pupils dilate fully, whites yellowish)
- Skin: Pale, slight sheen (sweat? or beginning of transformation?)
- Behavior: Twitchy, confused (higher brain shutting down, reptilian taking over)
- Clothing: Intact (recently infected, still wearing what they had)
- Threat level: MEDIUM (not fully aggressive yet, but unpredictable)

**STAGE 2: FULL INFECTION (24+ hours)**
- Appearance: Eyes BLACK (no whites visible, full dilation + darkness)
- Skin: Grayish, veins visible (dark purple/green under surface - bio-contamination)
- Biomechanical elements: NOT metal prosthetics - ORGANIC GROWTHS
  - Tendril-like structures emerge from spine (Giger xenomorph aesthetic)
  - Skin splits at stress points (elbows, knees) - bone/cartilage visible
  - Teeth sharpen (not fangs, but worn down to points - grinding)
- Movement: WRONG (joints bend slightly too far, gait is OFF - uncanny valley)
- Behavior: HUNTING (reptilian brain optimized for survival - aggressive, territorial)
- Clothing: Torn, stained (no longer cared for - self-maintenance gone)
- Threat level: EXTREME (touch = infection, will pursue relentlessly)

**STAGE 3: LATE INFECTION (weeks/months? - rare, environmental)**
- Appearance: FUSED with environment (Giger biomechanical landscape)
- Body: Partially merged with walls, floors (organic growths anchor them)
- State: Semi-dormant (low energy, but REACTIVE to movement nearby)
- Visual: Pulsing biomass, veins spread across surfaces, bioluminescent patches (sickly green glow)
- Threat: AREA DENIAL (these zones are INFECTED TERRITORY - avoid or clear)

### Key Visual Elements (Giger Aesthetic)

**1. BIOMECHANICAL FUSION:**
- Infected tissue grows CABLES (organic, pulsing - blood vessels? nerves?)
- Cables connect infected to each other (hive implications? shared reptilian instinct?)
- Cables spread across walls (environment becomes infected - spatial horror)

**2. BIOLUMINESCENCE:**
- Infected zones GLOW faintly (green/purple - visible in darkness)
- Function: WARNING (player can see danger), BEAUTY (horrifying but mesmerizing)
- Light pulses with breath/heartbeat (alive, organic)

**3. WETNESS:**
- Infected areas always WET (mucus? amniotic fluid? condensation?)
- Surfaces glisten, drip (visceral, disgusting)
- Sound: Wet breathing, dripping, squelching footsteps

**4. FACES:**
- MOST IMPORTANT: Faces remain recognizable (this WAS a person)
- Eyes: Black, empty (windows to soul now vacant)
- Expression: NEUTRAL or slight aggression (no human emotion, only reptilian reaction)
- Horror: You might recognize someone (NPC who got infected - personal stakes)

### Infected Movement (Animation Direction)

**Weaver/Biehn (motion capture notes):**
- NOT shambling zombies (too slow, not threatening in VR)
- IS: Twitchy, predatory, FAST when triggered
- Idle state: Swaying slightly (breathing, scanning - reptilian awareness)
- Alert state: Head snaps toward sound (bird-of-prey precision)
- Chase state: SPRINT (full-body commitment, no hesitation, TERRIFYING)
- Attack: Reaching, grasping (TOUCH = death, so hands are weapons)

**Critical:** Infected must be READABLE from distance (visual identification = survival skill)
- Silhouette: Hunched, head forward (predatory posture)
- Gait: Uneven, limping slightly (body damaged but functional)
- Sound: Breathing (raspy, wet), footsteps (irregular rhythm)

## IV. JOE BABSKY & PINEA - CHARACTER DESIGN

### Joe Babsky (Michael Biehn archetype)

**Reference:** Kyle Reese (Terminator), Dwayne Hicks (Aliens) - soldier survivor, not superhero

**Physical design (Rosinski + Fabry):**
- Age: 40s (weathered, experienced)
- Build: Lean, wiry (endurance over bulk - survival body, not gym body)
- Face: Scars (vertical line across left cheek - blade? shrapnel?), stubble (no time/resources for grooming), eyes TIRED (seen too much)
- Hair: Short, practical (military habit), graying at temples
- Prosthetics: LEFT ARM - crude mech (exposed metal, functional - he chose utility over vanity)
  - Arm design: Pistons visible, wrist rotates with audible click, fingers = 3 (thumb + 2 fingers, not full 5 - simplified)
  - Wear: Scratched, dented (he's USED this arm hard)

**Clothing:**
- Angloyankee faction colors: Olive drab green (#4B5320), faded camo (digital pattern, US military surplus?)
- Vest: Tactical (pouches, worn straps), patched (repairs visible - resource scarcity)
- Boots: Combat style, mud-caked, laces frayed
- No helmet (sees it as obstruction - trusts instincts over armor)

**Personality (visible in design):**
- Weapons: Maintained PERFECTLY (rifle clean, knife sharp - professional)
- Gear: Organized (every pouch has purpose, no wasted space)
- Posture: Alert, shoulders back (military discipline never leaves)
- Eyes: Constantly scanning (threat assessment automatic)

**Motion capture notes (Biehn):**
- Economy of movement (no wasted energy - every action deliberate)
- Weapon handling: Professional (reload without looking, finger discipline)
- Fatigue: Visible in idle (shoulders sag slightly, rubs face when thinking)
- Combat: Efficient, not flashy (double-tap, move to cover, assess)

**Voice/Dialogue delivery:**
- Tone: Calm, controlled (even under stress - trained response)
- Accent: American (Angloyankee), slight Southern drawl (Texas? Georgia?)
- Vocabulary: Military slang, practical (no philosophizing - action-oriented)

### Pinea (Sigourney Weaver archetype)

**Reference:** Ripley (Alien), Dana Barrett (Ghostbusters) - strong, intelligent, not defined by男性 gaze

**Physical design (Rosinski + Fabry):**
- Age: Late 30s (younger than Joe, but no damsel - equal partner)
- Build: Athletic, tall (Weaver's height - commands space)
- Face: High cheekbones (Slavic features?), intelligent eyes (curiosity, not fear), no makeup (post-apo practicality)
- Hair: Long, dark (braided - Native American influence from her fascination), beads woven in (cultural appreciation, not appropriation)
- Prosthetics: RIGHT LEG - bio-mimic (she chose to "pass" as unaugmented - vanity? or strategy?)
  - Leg design: Synthetic skin, nearly invisible (but player can notice slight stiffness in gait)
  - Emotional subtext: She HIDES her prosthetic (shame? or tactical advantage - enemies underestimate her?)

**Clothing:**
- Independent style (NOT matching Joe's military aesthetic - her own identity)
- Base: Leather jacket (brown, fringed - Native American inspired), worn but cared for
- Pants: Cargo style (practical), knee pads (she MOVES - climbing, crawling)
- Boots: Hiking style (ankle support, grip - she navigates vertical terrain)
- Accessories: Beaded bracelet (handmade?), leather pouch (herbs? tools? - cultural connection)

**Personality (visible in design):**
- Weapons: Bow (choice is DELIBERATE - silent, requires skill, cultural connection to Native American archery)
  - Bow design: Compound modern, but decorated (carved symbols, feathers tied to limbs)
- Gear: Eclectic (mix of salvaged tech + handmade items - resourceful, creative)
- Posture: Open, curious (leans IN to inspect things, not away - scientist mindset)
- Eyes: Wide, observant (notices details Joe misses - complementary skillset)

**Motion capture notes (Weaver):**
- Confident stride (long legs, no hesitation - claims space)
- Curiosity: Reaches out to touch/examine (scientist instinct - but cautious, not reckless)
- Combat: Precise, patient (bow requires setup - she chooses positions, waits for shot)
- Relationship with Joe: EQUAL (not behind him, not in front - beside,互补)

**Voice/Dialogue delivery:**
- Tone: Warm, but firm (empathy + strength)
- Accent: Neutral (educated? or deliberately code-switching?)
- Vocabulary: Mix of technical (science background?) + spiritual (Native American philosophy influence)
- Humor: Dry, sarcastic (matches Joe's pragmatism with wit)

### Joe + Pinea TOGETHER (visual dynamics)

**Height difference:** Pinea nearly same height as Joe (Weaver/Biehn are close in height - visual equality)

**Color palette contrast:**
- Joe: Greens, grays (military, blends in)
- Pinea: Browns, earth tones (organic, stands out slightly)
- Together: Complementary (not matching - individual identities)

**Prosthetics contrast:**
- Joe: VISIBLE mech arm (accepts/embraces augmentation)
- Pinea: HIDDEN bio-leg (conceals augmentation)
- Subtext: Different relationships with body modification (Joe = tool, Pinea = identity conflict?)

**Blocking (Ridley Scott direction):**
- NOT "man protects woman" (too cliche)
- IS: "Partners cover each other" (Joe watches right, Pinea watches left - trained coordination)
- Intimacy: NO TOUCH (COVID-X world = physical contact is DEATH)
  - Love shown through: Proximity (standing close, but not touching), eye contact (prolonged, meaningful), verbal affirmation (words replace physical affection)
  - Tragedy: They CANNOT touch, even as married couple (ontological horror of plague)

## VII. CINEMATIC MOMENTS (Key Visual Beats)

**Ridley Scott - Director's Shot List**

### TOP 10 CINEMATIC MOMENTS (Priority for visual polish)

**1. LIMBO OPENING (Phase 0)**
- **Shot:** High-angle descent into pod chamber, glass ceiling reflects stars
- **Duration:** 30 seconds (slow, dreamlike)
- **Mood:** Awe + isolation (birth/death transition)

**4. SAW APPROACHES (Phase 6, surgery)**
- **Shot:** Extreme close-up, saw blade rotating (out of focus), player's limb in foreground (sharp focus)
- **Duration:** 3 seconds (builds tension, then CUT TO BLACK)

## IX. MOTION CAPTURE NOTES (Acting Direction)

### PINEA (Sigourney Weaver)

**Character essence:** Curious scientist meets spiritual seeker. Strength without hardness. Warmth despite apocalypse.

**Physical traits:**
- **Stance:** Open (shoulders back, chest forward - confidence, not defensiveness)
- **Gait:** Long strides (tall, claims space), but SLIGHT limp (prosthetic leg - only noticeable if you watch closely)
- **Hands:** Active (gestures when talking - Italian energy, not restrained), touches objects (curiosity - examines textures, tools)
- **Eyes:** Wide, observant (takes IN information), frequent eye contact (engages with player/NPCs - empathy)

**Emotional beats:**
- **Joy:** Full-body (laughs with shoulders, not just face - genuine)
- **Fear:** Controlled (breath quickens, but posture stays strong - trained response)
- **Anger:** Focused (narrows eyes, jaw sets, but voice stays calm - dangerous when quiet)
- **Love (toward Joe):** Proximity (stands close, but NO touch - COVID-X world), prolonged eye contact (says with eyes what hands cannot)

**Combat:**
- **Bow draw:** Smooth, practiced (muscle memory), holds breath (focus), releases (slight shoulder rock - recoil)
- **Reload:** Fluid (arrow from quiver without looking), maintains awareness (eyes scan while reloading)
- **Stealth:** Patient (crouches low, weight on toes, slow movements - hunter, not soldier)

**Key direction:** Pinea is NOT "strong female character" (trope). She is PERSON who is strong. Vulnerability is not weakness (shows fear, but acts anyway). Curiosity drives her (wants to understand Infected, not just kill - scientist instinct).

### JOE BABSKY (Michael Biehn)

**Character essence:** Soldier who survived too long. Competent, not confident. Tired, but won't stop.

**Physical traits:**
- **Stance:** Bladed (right shoulder forward - instinctive combat posture), weight on balls of feet (ready to move)
- **Gait:** Economical (no wasted energy), quiet (heel-toe walk - stealth habit), prosthetic arm swings less (asymmetry subtle but present)
- **Hands:** Still (doesn't fidget - discipline), weapon always within reach (right hand drifts to rifle when stressed)
- **Eyes:** Constantly scanning (threat assessment automatic), rarely blinks (hyper-vigilance), looks AT people not away (direct, confrontational)

**Emotional beats:**
- **Joy:** Rare (small smile, corner of mouth only - doesn't trust happiness)
- **Fear:** Suppressed (jaw tightens, breath slows - controlled response, but it's THERE)
- **Anger:** Cold (no shouting - voice drops, words precise - more dangerous when calm)
- **Love (toward Pinea):** Protective proximity (stands between her and threats, but NOT "in front" - beside, covering flank), soft eyes (only person he looks at WITH softness)

**Combat:**
- **Weapon handling:** Professional (reload without looking, trigger discipline - finger off trigger until ready)
- **Movement:** Tactical (cover to cover, checks corners, never runs straight - trained)
- **Prosthetic arm use:** FUNCTIONAL (uses it for support/bracing rifle, no hesitation - accepted as part of body)

**Key direction:** Joe is NOT stoic hero (no emotion). He FEELS (fear, love, exhaustion), but CONTROLS expression (military training). Vulnerability shows in SMALL moments (rubs face when tired, hesitates before speaking about past). He is GOOD at survival, but NOT immune to cost.

### INFECTED (Ensemble - multiple actors)

**Movement essence:** WRONG. Not zombie shuffle, not human sprint. Something BETWEEN.

**Physical traits:**
- **Stance:** Hunched (spine curved forward), head tilted (listening? or neck muscles damaged?)
- **Gait:** Uneven (one leg drags slightly, but still FAST), arms swing asymmetrically (natural coordination GONE)
- **Breathing:** Audible (raspy, wet - damaged respiratory system, but functional)

**Behavioral states:**

**IDLE (not alerted):**
- Swaying (weight shifts foot to foot - restless)
- Head turns (scanning - reptilian awareness)
- Occasional twitch (muscle spasm - nervous system degrading)
- NO vocalization (silence is EERIE - no human sounds left)

**ALERT (heard something):**
- Head SNAPS toward sound (bird-of-prey precision - FAST)
- Body freezes (listening - full attention)
- Breath STOPS (moment of stillness - then...)

**CHASE (target acquired):**
- SPRINT (full-body commitment, no hesitation)
- Arms REACH forward (grasping - trying to touch)
- Footfalls HEAVY (no stealth, pure aggression)
- NO fatigue (reptilian brain doesn't feel exhaustion same way)

**ATTACK (within range):**
- Lunge (arms extended, hands open - GRABBING not punching)
- If miss: Stumble, recover quickly (clumsy but relentless)
- If contact: FREEZE (infection transfers - they STOP, job done)

**Key direction:** Infected are NOT mindless. They HUNT. Reptilian brain is cunning (ambush, flank, pursue). Movement should be UNSETTLING (almost human, but WRONG - uncanny valley). Actors should study predators (big cats, raptors) not zombies.

## X. AUDIO-VISUAL SYNERGY NOTES

**Critical:** VR relies on audio + visual TOGETHER. These moments require tight coordination:

**1. SAW SEQUENCE (Phase 6):**
- **Visual:** Saw blade rotating (close-up, slight motion blur)
- **Audio:** Metallic whine (rising pitch as RPM increases)
- **Haptic:** Controller vibration (rumble synced to audio pitch)
- **Timing:** 3-second buildup, then CUT (visual + audio both cut simultaneously - shock)

**2. INFECTED APPROACH:**
- **Visual:** Bioluminescent glow (green) in darkness ahead
- **Audio:** Wet breathing (off-screen, directional - player locates by sound)
- **Haptic:** Heartbeat pulse (player's fear response - increases when Infected close)
- **Timing:** Glow visible first (5 seconds), then breathing audible (3 seconds), then movement visible (1 second), then ALERT (head snap)

**3. ATMOSPHERIC ENTRY (Phase 9):**
- **Visual:** Orange glow (window edges), vibration (camera shake), clouds rushing past
- **Audio:** Roar (wind + friction), creaking (capsule stress), alarms (beeping)
- **Haptic:** Intense vibration (both controllers, constant), SLAM (impact pulse)
- **Timing:** 20-second sequence (build from gentle to violent), ends with SLAM + black screen

**4. WATER WARNING:**
- **Visual:** Still water surface (reflective, dark)
- **Audio:** SILENCE (no water sounds - eerie stillness)
- **Haptic:** NONE (absence of feedback = danger signal)
- **Timing:** Player approaches water, sounds FADE OUT (ambient noise dies - warning)

## XI. TECHNICAL ART DIRECTION

### VR-Specific Visual Considerations

**1. DEPTH PERCEPTION (stereoscopic):**
- **Orbital scenes:** Use glass reflections (depth layers - glass, reflection, space beyond)
- **Infected zones:** Bioluminescence in BG, Infected in MID, player in FG (clear threat distance)
- **Flooded Warsaw:** Water surface (reflective) separates air/underwater (visual boundary critical)

**2. SCALE (VR amplifies):**
- **Pods (Phase 0):** Must feel LARGE (player small, insignificant - existential)
- **Earth from orbit:** Must feel VAST (window frame provides scale reference)
- **Infected:** Human-sized (NOT giants - relatable horror, "this was a person")

**3. MOTION SICKNESS MITIGATION:**
- **Capsule descent:** Camera shake MODERATE (not excessive - nausea risk)
- **Player movement:** Smooth acceleration (no instant velocity changes)
- **Horizons:** Always visible (grounds player - sky/water line in flooded Warsaw critical)

**4. READABILITY (VR resolution limits):**
- **UI text:** Large, high-contrast (white on black, or cyan on black - neZUS style)
- **Infected silhouettes:** Distinct from humans (hunched posture readable at distance)
- **Navigation:** Biper glow (bright green arrow) visible in peripheral vision (player doesn't need to look directly at hand)

## XII. FINAL VISUAL PHILOSOPHY

**Siudmak (summarizing team vision):**

PLAGA'44 is NOT about showing apocalypse. It's about showing what comes AFTER.

- **Orbital:** Humanity's best technology (neZUS immortality) is cold, transactional, corporate. Beauty without soul.
- **Infected:** Humanity's worst nightmare (loss of self) is organic, intimate, inevitable. Horror is personal.
- **Surface:** Humanity's stubborn survival (flooded Warsaw) is ugly, beautiful, tragic, hopeful. Life persists in ruins.

**Visual balance:**
- 30% sterile (orbital - clean, oppressive)
- 20% horror (infected zones - visceral, terrifying)
- 50% survival (surface - varied, textured, LIVED IN)

**Emotional arc:**
- Character creation: Disempowerment (you are product, not person)
- Landing: Isolation (you are alone, vulnerable)
- Gameplay: Agency (you survive, resist, CHOOSE)

**Final image (core visual identity):**
Imagine: Player stands on rooftop in flooded Warsaw. Behind: Infected zone (green glow, horror). Below: Water (gray, death). Above: Orbital station (tiny dot, uncaring). Ahead: Another survivor (Joe? Pinea?), reaching out hand (but cannot touch).

This is PLAGA'44. Beauty in impossible survival. Hope despite ontological horror. Humanity when humanity itself is redefined.
