LOCOMOTION (LocomotionController.cs -- REWRITE)
================================================

  R THUMBSTICK Y-AXIS:

    GROUNDED ──[stick UP]──> ASCENDING
      _flySpeed += flyAcceleration * _dt
      _verticalVelocity = _flySpeed (accelerating upward)
      gravity OFF

    ASCENDING ──[release]──> HOVERING
      _verticalVelocity = _hoverDrift (gentle random +-0.3 m/s)
      drift target changes every 1-3 sec, smooth lerp
      gravity OFF -- player floats

    HOVERING ──[stick UP]──> ASCENDING (boost again)
    HOVERING ──[stick DOWN]──> GROUNDED (gravity returns, fall)
    any ──[land]──> GROUNDED

  STANCE (on ground only):

    DOWN short tap: STAND <-> CROUCH
    DOWN long hold (>0.6s): -> PRONE
    UP flick from crouch/prone: -> STAND

    CC height:        1.8 / 1.0 / 0.5
    CC center.y:      h * 0.5
    TrackingSpace.y:  baseY - (standHeight - targetHeight)
                      ^^^ camera drops visually in VR


PAUSE MOVEMENT (GameState.cs)
==============================

  GameState.CanMove = Playing || Paused
  GameState.PlayerDeltaTime:
    Playing -> Time.deltaTime
    Paused  -> Time.unscaledDeltaTime

  LocomotionController: _dt = GameState.PlayerDeltaTime
  SmoothTurnController: GameState.PlayerDeltaTime

  EFFECT: player moves during pause, world frozen (timeScale=0)
